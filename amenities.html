  • Amenities

  • Restaurants: Taniti currently has five Restaurants that serve mostly local fish and rice, three that serve American-style meals, and two that serve Pan-Asian cuisine. Note: Alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold between the hours of midnight and 9:00 a.m. The drinking age on Taniti is 18, though this is not strictly enforced.

  • Grocery Stores: Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store that is open 24 hours a day. Note: Taniti uses the U.S. dollar as its currency, but many businesses will also accept euros and yen. Additionally, several banks facilitate currency exchange.
